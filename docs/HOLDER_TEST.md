# Holder test (for a real Chog holder)

Message to post in Chog's Discord, with your live link:

> Building ChogBound for Chogathon: a daily Chog coaching game where your Chog's real traits decide the fight. I need one or two holders to test the wallet flow. Takes 2 minutes: open [LIVE URL], tap Pick, then Connect Wallet. It only asks for a signature (no transaction, no gas). Tell me what you see. Thank you!

## What the tester should do

1. Open the link on the device they normally use (phone wallet browser or desktop with a wallet extension).
2. Pick, then CONNECT WALLET. Approve the connection and the signature request.
3. Check that the Chogs listed are exactly the Chogs in their wallet, with the right art and name.
4. Choose one, lock a plan on Coach, then run tonight's fight and watch the replay.

## What to report back

- Wallet used and device.
- Did the Monad network switch work? Any prompt that looked wrong?
- Did the list match the wallet? Missing or extra Chogs?
- Any error message, and a screenshot.
- Anything confusing or slow.

Do not share a seed phrase or private key. The game never needs one.
