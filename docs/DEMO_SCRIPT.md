# 3 minute demo script

Hard limit 3:00. Record at phone size or a narrow browser window; keep the cursor slow.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home page | "ChogBound is a daily coaching game for Chog NFTs. Your Chog's real traits decide how it fights." |
| 0:15 | Pick page, wallet panel | "Holders connect a wallet and play with the Chogs they own. Anyone else can play as a guest." |
| 0:30 | Holder cut-in (see below) | "This is a real holder: connect, sign, and their own Chogs appear." |
| 0:55 | Coach page, trait sheet | "Every trait is mapped. This Chog's eyes give it PUNCH, its aura gives a fight effect. Different Chog, different fight." |
| 1:15 | Lock a plan | "I choose temperament, a move and how much risk to take." |
| 1:30 | Replay, playing with voice | "Tonight's fight is a real simulator log. Each hit is one voiced line, and effects show as chips." |
| 2:00 | Skip to result | "The result stays hidden until the fight ends." |
| 2:10 | Arena | "Every Chog fights one rival each night, drawn from all 1,969. Standings use autopilot plans, so anyone can recompute them." |
| 2:35 | Roadmap or repo | "Next: an on-chain record contract and token-bound accounts. Code and tests are in the repository." |
| 2:50 | Title card | "ChogBound. Your Chog. Your plan. Your fight." |

## Holder cut-in

You do not hold a Chog, so ask a holder to screen-record 20 seconds of the connect flow (Pick, Connect Wallet, sign, their Chogs appear) and cut it in at 0:30. Do not fake it. If no holder is available, say plainly in the video that the wallet flow is shown with a test wallet, and show the Pick page in guest mode.

## Before recording

- Use the live URL, not localhost.
- Check the replay voice toggle: speech needs a browser with an installed voice.
- Do a dry run to confirm the whole video is under 3 minutes.
